Academy overview | Document ID: RLA-OVERVIEW-01

ABOUT THE ACADEMY
Riverlight Learning Academy is a fictional training center in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
It teaches practical technology skills through small classes and project work.
Its campus address is 18 Learning Lane, Dhanmondi, Dhaka.
The academy was founded in 2022 by Nila Rahman.

OFFICE AND CONTACT
The administration office is open Sunday through Thursday, 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
The office is closed on Friday and Saturday.
All times in academy documents are local time in Dhaka, Bangladesh.
General inquiries go to hello@riverlight.example.
Course enrollment inquiries go to admissions@riverlight.example.
The academy website is https://riverlight.example.
The .example addresses are fictional and are not working contact channels.

LEARNING APPROACH
Each course includes instructor demonstrations, individual practice, and a final
project. Students bring their own laptops. Classroom Wi-Fi is available.
The academy has a computer lab with 12 workstations, but lab seats must be booked
through the administration office at least one working day in advance.
The lab is available Sunday through Thursday from 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM.
These are computer lab hours; library hours are listed on the library notice.

COURSE DOCUMENTS
The structured course catalog lists course IDs, fees, durations, prerequisites,
and instructors. The course guide explains the enrollment process and class
schedules. The student handbook defines attendance and refund rules.
The library notice is provided as an image so that learners can practice OCR.

STUDENT SUPPORT
Students can request one 20-minute academic support session each week.
Requests go to support@riverlight.example at least 24 hours before the session.
The academy provides digital course materials after enrollment is confirmed.
Recorded sessions, when available, remain accessible for 60 days after a course ends.
The academy does not provide accommodation or transport.
